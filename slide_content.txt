Abstract | 3 | Informational estimate only — not medical or dietary advice. | Contemporary Challenge: | Meal-photo apps return one single calorie number for a whole plate; a mixed Indian meal (rice, dal, chapati, sabzi) makes that single number wrong by construction. | Proposed System: | From a single meal photograph, decompose the plate into individual components, estimate mass geometrically in grams, and retrieve verified nutrients from IFCT 2017 / USDA. | Strict Grounding Rule: | The vision-language model is restricted to perception only (names, regions, physical states); it NEVER produces calories, grams, or macro numbers (BR-01). | Calibrated Uncertainty: | A fused 7-signal reliability score calibrated via isotonic regression; low-confidence components trigger active, targeted user clarification instead of silent guessing. | Indian-First Coverage: | Dual-index RAG over IFCT 2017 with physical-state filtering — cooked rice matches 130 kcal/100 g, avoiding the 345 kcal/100 g raw rice error. | Evaluation Targets: | Benchmarked on gold_v1 (150 images of 60 weighed Indian meals); targets component F1 ≥ 0.80, kcal MAPE ≤ 18 %, ECE ≤ 0.10, latency ≤ 20 s. | Core contribution: | Decomposing calorie error into identity × mass × grounding × calibration — then solving each stage with measurable, verifiable components.

Motivation of Work | 9 | Informational estimate only — not medical or dietary advice. | The error is multiplicative: | Total calories = identity × mass. Fixing one and guessing the other caps accuracy no matter how capable the underlying model is. | The failure is measurable: | Commercial apps report 550 kcal for a thali plate that actually contains: rice (215) + dal (140) + chapati (210) + sabzi (100) = 665 kcal — a hidden 17 % error. | Indian plates are our primary benchmark: | Thali, biryani, sambar-rice, upma, idli, poha, curd rice — IFCT 2017 covers them, but mainstream consumer apps fail to leverage it. | Uncertainty must be transparent: | Displaying ranges and calibrated confidence bands is honest; a point estimate falsely implies precision a 2D photograph cannot support. | Deployable on commodity hardware: | Runs on a standard CPU-only laptop using SQLite + Chroma, with a deterministic mock mode ensuring complete examiner reproducibility. | A clear academic and clinical gap: | No published system unifies component decomposition, database-grounded retrieval, calibrated confidence, and targeted clarification. | Core Research Question: | Can decomposing meal components, grounding each in composition databases, and clarifying only when uncertain beat a confident one-shot guess?

Requirements | 15 | Informational estimate only — not medical or dietary advice. | System Specifications: Core Functional Capabilities and Non-Functional Engineering Standards | Functional Requirements (System Capabilities) | Multi-Component Segmentation: Decomposes mixed plates into 3–8 discrete items with bounding regions, physical states (raw, cooked, gravy, fried), and visibility flags. The VLM emits labels and coordinates only, never calorie numbers or grams. | Scale-Anchored Portioning: Detects plate diameter or reference object to establish real-world scale (px/cm); computes projected area, applies category volume/heap factors, and maps density to grams with an 80 % log-normal confidence interval. | Knowledge-Base Retrieval (RAG): Matches components against IFCT 2017 & USDA FDC using hybrid dense (bge-small) + BM25 search with Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF, k=60); enforces physical-state filtering and rejects matches below 0.55 similarity. | Deterministic Nutrition Arithmetic: Pure Python calculation scaling database nutrients by estimated mass outside the LLM (kJ ÷ 4.184 = kcal); missing values remain null and are never coerced to zero. | Critic & Plausibility Validation: Evaluates physical plausibility, mass balance, and macronutrient density to veto impossible predictions before presenting to the user. | Calibrated Active Clarification: Fuses 7 pipeline signals into a calibrated reliability score; triggers up to 2 targeted questions per round (max 2 rounds) for ambiguous components. | Persistence & Audit Trail: Persists complete execution provenance (model, prompt version, KB version, compute version) in SQLite/Chroma for 100 % deterministic re-runs. | Non-Functional Requirements (Quality & Constraints) | Accuracy & Calibration Invariants: Must achieve component F1 ≥ 0.80, caloric MAPE ≤ 18 %, calorie MAE ≤ 90 kcal, and Expected Calibration Error (ECE ≤ 0.10) on weighed benchmark plates. | Performance & Cost Budgets: Fast interactive execution targeting p50 latency ≤ 8 s and p95 ≤ 20 s on complex plates; compute API cost capped at ≤ $0.01 per meal analysis. | Offline Autonomy & Mock Mode: MOCK_MODE=1 executes the entire pipeline offline without internet access or paid API keys, returning byte-for-byte identical, reproducible outputs for evaluation. | Grounding Integrity & Zero Hallucination: 100 % of non-null displayed nutrients must trace back to published composition database records; zero generative AI in the calculation path. | Commodity Hardware Deployment: Runs CPU-only on a standard Windows laptop with 8 GB RAM using local SQLite (WAL mode) and Chroma storage; cold-start time ≤ 60 s. | Safety Scope & Ethical Disclaimer: Informational self-tracking only; displays strict non-medical disclaimer on all views with zero clinical, prescription, or diagnostic claims. | Data Privacy & Retention: User meal photographs and nutritional records are stored locally by default with full user-controlled deletion on demand. | Architectural Guarantee: | Functional stages execute the perception-to-report pipeline; non-functional constraints enforce factual grounding, offline demoability, and statistical honesty.
